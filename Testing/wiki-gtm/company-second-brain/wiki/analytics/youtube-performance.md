# YouTube Performance

## Summary
Over the 30 days from 2026-06-11 to 2026-07-10, the MLAI Community YouTube
channel grew from 8,211 to 8,708 subscribers — a net gain of 497 (+6.1%) — while
serving 78,053 views and 5,411 watch hours. Growth was steady rather than
spiky: no single day added more than 33 subscribers, and the channel never lost
subscribers on any day in the window. Views followed a clear two-mode pattern,
alternating between ~1,000–1,500 view days and ~3,000–4,200 view days, which
tracks publishing cadence rather than virality. Engagement is the standout
number: at 5.34% (likes + comments over views), the channel out-engages all four
tracked competitors despite being smaller than three of them.

## Notable Days

- **2026-06-26 — 4,197 views, peak of the window.** The highest-view day of the
  period, but it produced only 6 subscribers gained, one of the weakest
  conversion days. High-traffic, low-conversion suggests a discovery or Shorts
  surface rather than an intent-driven viewer.
- **2026-07-05 — 913 views but 33 subscribers gained.** The inverse case, and
  the single best views-to-subscriber ratio in the window. This is the day
  Cohort 9's completion was posted on LinkedIn (`LINKEDIN_POSTS` row 1, 312
  reactions — the highest-performing post in the table). Cross-channel referral
  from an engaged LinkedIn audience is the plausible explanation.
- **2026-07-03 — 3,686 views, only 4 subscribers gained.** The lowest subscriber
  gain of the window. Two comments landed on *AI Product Manager Roadmap 2026*
  this day: one converting viewer who cited the RAG section, and one asking
  whether the pricing is justified (`YOUTUBE_COMMENTS` rows 3, 4). See
  [[pricing-objections]].
- **2026-07-09 — avg view duration falls to 171 sec, the floor for the window**,
  against 1,880 views. Retention, not traffic, was the problem that day.
- **2026-06-23 — 328.9 watch hours, the peak.** Driven by both high views
  (3,774) and the second-highest average view duration (314 sec).
