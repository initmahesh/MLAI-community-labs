# LinkedIn Performance

## Summary
Between 2026-06-11 and 2026-07-10 the MLAI Community LinkedIn page grew from
5,606 to 5,958 followers, a net gain of 352 (+6.3%) — almost exactly matching
the YouTube channel's growth rate over the same window. The page served 164,514
impressions and 3,086 clicks. Unlike YouTube, LinkedIn shows no correlation
between reach and follower acquisition: the highest-impression days converted
poorly, while several low-impression days produced the largest follower gains.
The five posts in `LINKEDIN_POSTS` are dominated by third-party advocacy —
students and alumni posting about the program — rather than owned marketing,
and those advocacy posts substantially outperform the one competitor
advertisement in the table.

## Notable Days

- **2026-06-16 — 2,282 impressions but 22 followers gained.** The best
  conversion day of the window, on the second-lowest reach. Small, targeted
  reach converting well is a recurring pattern on this page.
- **2026-06-12 — 8,946 impressions, the peak, for only 18 followers.** Broad
  reach that did not convert proportionally.
- **2026-07-09 — 448 engagements and 197 clicks on 7,563 impressions**, the
  strongest combined engagement day. This is four days after the Cohort 9
  completion post (`LINKEDIN_POSTS` row 1, 312 reactions) and five days after
  Sunil Gangavarapu's certification post (row 2, 187 reactions); sustained
  comment activity on those threads is the likely driver. See [[cohort-9]].
- **2026-07-10 — 1,864 impressions, the floor.** Also the final day of the
  reporting window, so the figure may be partial rather than a genuine drop.
  Flagged as an open question below.
- **2026-06-22 — Kartiki Saraf's post (row 4, 156 reactions)** singled out the
  Snowflake + Claude lab as the bootcamp highlight. Page impressions that day
  were a modest 3,836, suggesting the post's reach accrued to her personal
  network rather than the company page.

  cannot be tied to daily page metrics with confidence.
