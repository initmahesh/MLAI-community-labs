# Hardcore Agentic Engineering

## Summary
Hardcore Agentic Engineering runs *Hardcore Agentic Engineering for Builders* on
Maven, taught by Godhuli Chatterjee. It is the smallest channel in the tracked
set and the least directly competitive: it deliberately targets engineers who
want to build agentic products rather than product managers. Its reviews
confirm the positioning is working — and reveal the cost of it.

## Key Facts
- Course: *Hardcore Agentic Engineering for Builders*, on Maven

- Instructor: **Godhuli Chatterjee** — 18 years at Amazon, Google, and Microsoft
  (`COMPETITORS` row 4).
- Positioning: aimed at engineers building agentic products, explicitly *not* at

- Peak views day: 2026-06-20, 1,785 views.
- Maven sentiment: 2 reviews, average rating **4.0** — the lowest of any tracked

  but light on the product-management side they had hoped for
  (`COMPETITOR_MAVEN_FEEDBACK` rows 7, 8).

## Competitive Read
They occupy the far technical end of the spectrum, and their split reviews map
the boundary precisely: engineers are delighted, PMs are underserved. That
disappointed technical PM is our buyer. Our program's stated value is that it
"equips you both technically and functionally as an AI PM"
(`MAVEN_FEEDBACK` row 3, short quoted phrase), which is the exact gap their
3-star reviewer describes.

The mirror-image risk is worth stating plainly: our own reviewers have asked for
*more* PM content, not less. One noted that the PM mindset outlasts any
particular model release and wanted more time on it (`MAVEN_FEEDBACK` row 4,
private note, paraphrased). If we drift technical, we drift toward this
competitor's failure mode. See [[pm-vs-engineering-balance]].
