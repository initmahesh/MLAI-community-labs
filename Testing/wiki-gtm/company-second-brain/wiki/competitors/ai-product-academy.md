# AI Product Academy

## Summary
AI Product Academy runs the *AI Product Management Bootcamp & Certification* on
Maven, taught by Dr. Marily Nika. It is the most credentialed and most
established competitor in the tracked set — Maven's top-ranked AI PM
certification with roughly 100 cohorts delivered. Its reviewers consistently
admire the structure and the instructor's pedigree while asking for more
hands-on practice, which is precisely the axis on which our own reviews are
strongest.

## Key Facts
- Course: *AI Product Management Bootcamp & Certification*, on Maven
  (`COMPETITORS` row 1).
- Instructor: **Dr. Marily Nika** — ex-Google/Meta, PhD in ML, Harvard Business
  School fellow (`COMPETITORS` row 1).
- Positioning: Maven's top-ranked AI PM certification; ~100 cohorts run to date
  (`COMPETITORS` row 1). This is roughly 11× our cohort count — see [[cohort-9]].
- YouTube handle: `@marilynika` (`COMPETITORS` row 1).
- Subscriber trend over 2026-06-11 → 2026-07-10: **14,214 → 14,546**, a net gain
  of 332 (+2.34%). Raw `subscribers_gained` sums to 346, implying ~14
  unsubscribes (`COMPETITOR_YOUTUBE_ANALYTICS`, competitor_id 1).
- Averaged 5,576 daily views (2.1× ours) and a 4.79% engagement rate, second
  highest among competitors but below our 5.34%.
- Peak views day: 2026-07-09, 8,881 views.
- Maven sentiment: 2 reviews, average rating **4.5**. Praise centres on the
  structured lifecycle framework and instructor credibility. Both criticisms
  point the same direction — one reviewer wanted more hands-on coding practice,
  the other found it "slightly theory-heavy" against expectation
  (`COMPETITOR_MAVEN_FEEDBACK` rows 1, 2).

## Competitive Read
The theory-heavy criticism is the single most actionable competitor signal in
this dataset. Our reviewers praise exactly what theirs miss: practical,
hands-on labs (`MAVEN_FEEDBACK` rows 1, 6, 11). A prospect choosing between the
two is choosing credentials-and-structure versus build-it-yourself depth. See
[[hands-on-labs-praise]] and [[pm-vs-engineering-balance]].
