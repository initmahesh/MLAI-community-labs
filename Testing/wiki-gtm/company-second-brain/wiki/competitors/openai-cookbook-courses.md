# OpenAI Cookbook Courses

## Summary
Not a tracked competitor and not a course business — this page exists because a
prospect named it as an alternative in a YouTube comment, which makes it part of
our real consideration set regardless of whether it belongs in the `COMPETITORS`
table. It represents the "free first-party vendor documentation" substitute:
the option a cost-sensitive, self-directed learner reaches for instead of paying
for a cohort.

## Key Facts
- Named by commenter `competitor_watcher` on the video *Claude Code for
  Non-Engineers*, asking how our content compares to the OpenAI cookbook courses
  (`YOUTUBE_COMMENTS` row 8, posted 2026-06-20).
- The comment drew only 6 likes — the lowest of any comment in the table —
  suggesting it is not a widely shared concern among our audience
  (`YOUTUBE_COMMENTS` row 8).
- It appeared on the same video and same day as our most affirming comment: a
  self-described non-technical viewer saying the video made Claude Code feel
  approachable, at 54 likes, the second-highest in the table
  (`YOUTUBE_COMMENTS` row 7).
- **Absent from every competitor table.** No analytics, no reviews, no
  instructor, no pricing.

## Competitive Read
The substitute is free, first-party, and vendor-specific. It offers reference
material, not cohort structure, mentor access, evaluation, or a certification —
none of which the cookbook attempts to provide. Our reviewers consistently
attribute their outcomes to the things a cookbook cannot deliver: mentors
bending over backwards, an intense structured progression, and shipping a
working prototype (`MAVEN_FEEDBACK` rows 4, 9). The honest answer to this
prospect's question is that they solve different problems.

Also worth noting for positioning: the comment implicitly frames us as
Anthropic-aligned, given the video subject. Our curriculum teaches Claude Code
and MCP (`YOUTUBE_COMMENTS` rows 1, 7). Being read as tied to one vendor is a
risk if a prospect's employer has standardised on another.
