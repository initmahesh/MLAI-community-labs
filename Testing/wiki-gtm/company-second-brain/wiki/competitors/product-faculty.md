# Product Faculty

## Summary
Product Faculty runs the *AI Product Management Course (4D Method)* on Maven,
co-taught by Rohan Varma and Henry Shi. It is the closest competitor to us in
both size and strategy: a small channel that competes on builder credibility and
hands-on currency rather than reach. It is also the only competitor whose
reviews contain an explicit pricing complaint — making it the most useful
comparison point for our own pricing conversation.

## Key Facts
- Course: *AI Product Management Course (4D Method)*, on Maven
  (`COMPETITORS` row 3).
- Instructors: **Rohan Varma** (first PM at Cursor) and **Henry Shi** (Member of
  Technical Staff at Anthropic) (`COMPETITORS` row 3).
- Positioning: strong "builder credibility" angle; offers private team cohorts
  (`COMPETITORS` row 3). The team-cohort option is a B2B motion we do not

- Subscriber trend over 2026-06-11 → 2026-07-10: **6,103 → 6,254**, a net gain of

- Averaged 1,832 daily views (below our 2,602) with a 4.82% engagement rate —
  the highest of any competitor, though still short of our 5.34%.
- Peak views day: 2026-06-17, 2,818 views.
- Maven sentiment: 2 reviews, average rating **4.5**. Praise: the
  builder-credibility angle lands, and the material feels current and
  hands-on — one reviewer, an engineer moving into PM, said so directly.
  Criticism: pricier than alternatives, though the team-cohort option was
  received as a positive (`COMPETITOR_MAVEN_FEEDBACK` rows 5, 6).

## Competitive Read
This is our mirror image and our most direct threat. Both courses sell
hands-on depth over credentials-and-structure; both are small; both draw
technical audiences. Two asymmetries matter. First, their instructor bench
carries frontier-lab affiliation (Cursor, Anthropic) that reads instantly on a
landing page, while our credibility is earned inside the course — see
[[instructor-teaching-style]]. Second, they take pricing criticism at 4-star
level while every one of our public reviews is 5-star, suggesting our value
perception among enrolled students is currently stronger than theirs. The
unresolved question is whether that survives contact with a prospect who has not
yet enrolled — see [[pricing-objections]].

Their private team-cohort offering is worth noting: our student base is heavy
with Directors and Principal-level staff at named enterprises (see
[[tibco]], [[bank-ozk]], [[loblaw-companies]]), which is exactly the population
a team-cohort motion targets.

## Open Questions
- Do we offer team or enterprise cohorts? Nothing in the data indicates we do,
  and our roster suggests demand for one.
- Their pricing complaint says "pricier than alternatives" — pricier than whom?
  Without price points for any course, we cannot place ourselves on that axis.
