# Product Manager Accelerator

## Summary
Product Manager Accelerator runs the *AI PM Bootcamp* on Maven, taught by Dr.
Nancy Li. It is by far the largest channel in the tracked set — 121,605 YouTube
subscribers against our 8,708 — and competes on audience reach and a crisp
"become an AI PM in 90 days" promise rather than on technical depth. Its
weakness, per its own reviewers, is that scale costs intimacy: large cohorts
that feel impersonal.

## Key Facts
- Course: *AI PM Bootcamp*, on Maven (`COMPETITORS` row 2).
- Instructor: **Dr. Nancy Li** — LinkedIn Top Voice, 100K+ followers across
  social (`COMPETITORS` row 2).
- Positioning: a structured "become an AI PM in 90 days" narrative
  (`COMPETITORS` row 2) — outcome-and-timeline framing rather than
  curriculum-depth framing.
- YouTube handle: `@ProductManagerAccelerator` (`COMPETITORS` row 2).
- Subscriber trend over 2026-06-11 → 2026-07-10: **118,051 → 121,605**, a net
  gain of 3,554 (+3.01%) (`COMPETITOR_YOUTUBE_ANALYTICS`, competitor_id 2).
- **This is the scale problem in one line:** their 3.01% growth adds 3,554
  subscribers per month; our faster 6.05% adds 497.
- Averaged 12,615 daily views — 4.8× ours, and the highest in the set — on
  378,435 total views over the window.
- Lowest engagement rate of any tracked channel at **4.11%**, against our 5.34%.
  Broad reach, thinner engagement.
- Peak views day: 2026-07-03, 21,583 views.
- Maven sentiment: 2 reviews, average rating **4.5**. Praise focuses on the
  instructor's personal story being inspiring and the course suiting beginners.
  Criticism: the 90-day framing motivates, but the cohort felt large and less
  personal (`COMPETITOR_MAVEN_FEEDBACK` rows 3, 4).

## Competitive Read
They own top-of-funnel. We are not going to out-reach a 121K channel in this
window, and treating subscriber count as the scoreboard would be a mistake.
The opening is at the other end: their reviewers describe a beginner-oriented,
large-cohort experience, while ours describe intensity, mentor access, and
shipping a working prototype (`MAVEN_FEEDBACK` rows 4, 9). Different buyer,
overlapping search term. See [[hands-on-labs-praise]] and [[cohort-9]].

## Open Questions
- Their audience skews "aspiring AI PM" per review titles; ours skews senior
  practitioner (Directors and Principals — see [[tibco]], [[bank-ozk]],
  [[microsoft]]). Are we actually competing for the same buyer, or ranking
  against them by keyword only?
- No pricing data available; a beginner-oriented, high-volume course likely
  prices below us, which would sharpen [[pricing-objections]].
