# PM vs. Engineering Balance

## Summary
Every course in this market sits somewhere on a line between product theory and
technical build. Our claimed position is the middle — "both technically and
functionally as an AI PM", in a customer's own words. The data supports that we
hold it, and also contains a warning: our most credible reviewer says we are
drifting technical, and the competitor furthest along that drift is the only one
with a 3-star review.

## Key Facts
- [[loblaw-companies]]' Venkat krishnan Chellappa states the course equips a
  student "both technically and functionally as a AI PM" (`MAVEN_FEEDBACK`
  row 3, short quoted phrase). This is the cleanest articulation of our position
  and it came from a customer.
- [[bank-ozk]]'s Avinendra Chauhan is a **data engineer buying product
  thinking** — the inverse traveller (`MAVEN_FEEDBACK` row 8, `private_note`).
- [[hardcore-agentic-engineering]]'s 3-star reviewer is a technical PM who found
  it technically deep but light on the PM side they wanted
  (`COMPETITOR_MAVEN_FEEDBACK` row 8). **That person is our buyer.**
- [[ai-product-academy]]'s two reviewers both pull the opposite direction,
  wanting more hands-on practice (`COMPETITOR_MAVEN_FEEDBACK` rows 1, 2).
- Market demand, stated by a buyer: a Head of Product at a fintech startup asked
  publicly where to find AI PMs who understand RAG, evals, and agent
  architecture rather than buzzwords (`LINKEDIN_POSTS` row 5). That post carries
  the **highest comment count** of any post in the table (33) on the lowest
  reactions (62) — argument, not applause.

## The Warning
[[stanford]]'s Anja Lee — a Data PM who coaches and instructs — used her private
note to ask for **more PM skills content and more time to hone it**. Her
reasoning deserves to be quoted in substance rather than summarised away: not
everyone in the room is a PM, and the PM mindset matters *more* than any
technical content because it survives each new model release
(`MAVEN_FEEDBACK` row 4, `private_note`, paraphrased).

She is the most qualified person in our dataset to make that judgement, and she
made it privately while rating us 5 stars publicly. Read alongside
[[hardcore-agentic-engineering]]'s 3-star technical-PM review, the risk is
legible: the failure mode of a technically excellent AI PM course is that it
stops being a PM course.
