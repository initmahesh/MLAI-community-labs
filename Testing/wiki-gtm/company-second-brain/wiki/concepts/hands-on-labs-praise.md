# Hands-On Labs Praise

## Summary
The most consistent theme across our reviews is that the labs work. Practical,
applied, hands-on lab work is named by six of eleven reviewers, and it is the
single largest differentiator against our nearest credentialed competitor, whose
own reviewers ask for exactly what ours receive.

## The Competitive Wedge
This is the clearest exploitable gap in the dataset. [[ai-product-academy]] is
the top-ranked AI PM certification on Maven with ~100 cohorts and a PhD-credentialed
instructor — and **both** of its recorded reviewers ask for the thing we are
praised for. One wished for more hands-on coding practice; the other found it
slightly theory-heavy against expectation (`COMPETITOR_MAVEN_FEEDBACK`
rows 1, 2).

Meanwhile [[product-faculty]] competes on the same hands-on axis and earns
praise for it (`COMPETITOR_MAVEN_FEEDBACK` row 5). They are the real contest
here, not the market leader.

## The Counterweight
Praise for labs is not unqualified. [[stanford]]'s Anja Lee — herself an
instructor — recorded in a private note that the labs were being **changed while
students were working through them**, causing confusion
(`MAVEN_FEEDBACK` row 4, `private_note`, paraphrased). The labs are our strongest
asset and our only recorded operational defect. See
[[course-logistics-friction]].
