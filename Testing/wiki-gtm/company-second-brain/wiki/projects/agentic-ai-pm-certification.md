# Agentic AI PM Certification

## Summary
This is the product. It is a six-week, cohort-based, live AI Product Management
certification delivered on Maven by Mahesh with a supporting mentor team. It
appears in the data under at least three names, carries a perfect 5.0 rating
across eleven public reviews, and promises a shipped prototype rather than a
credential.

## Naming
The program is referred to inconsistently across sources, which matters for
search and for anyone querying this wiki:
- "Agentic AI PM Certification" — [[t-mobile]]'s Sunil Gangavarapu
  (`LINKEDIN_POSTS` row 2)
- "AIPM course" / "AI PM Course" — [[tibco]]'s Hiren Shah, [[microsoft]]'s
  Kartiki Saraf (`MAVEN_FEEDBACK` rows 11, 9)
- "the bootcamp" — [[microsoft]]'s Kartiki Saraf (`LINKEDIN_POSTS` row 4)
- "this specialization" — [[microsoft]]'s Kartiki Saraf
  (`MAVEN_FEEDBACK` row 9, `private_note`)

## Key Facts
- **Platform:** Maven — the same platform as all four tracked competitors
  (`COMPETITORS` rows 1–4). We compete inside their marketplace.
- **Duration:** six weeks (`LINKEDIN_POSTS` row 1).
- **Instructor:** Mahesh, Founder, MLAI Community (`LINKEDIN_POSTS` row 1),
  supported by mentors credited independently by two students
  (`MAVEN_FEEDBACK` rows 1, 4). See [[instructor-teaching-style]].
- **Rating:** 5.00 across 11 public reviews — every review is 5 stars
  (`MAVEN_FEEDBACK` rows 1–11). Compare a competitor average of 4.375
  (`COMPETITOR_MAVEN_FEEDBACK`), but see the caveat in
  [[course-logistics-friction]] about where criticism actually lives.
- **Stated outcome:** shipping a working prototype in six weeks
  (`LINKEDIN_POSTS` row 1), not a certificate.
- **Latest run:** [[cohort-9]], completed 2026-07-05.

## Who Buys It
Eleven reviewers: five practising PMs or Senior PMs, two data engineers, one
Program Manager, one Director, one Process Transformation Lead, one PM with no
recorded employer. **Three of the eleven are between roles.** Seniority skews
high — Directors and Principals appear repeatedly. See
[[career-transition-into-ai-pm]].

## Positioning
"Both technically and functionally as a AI PM" ([[loblaw-companies]],
`MAVEN_FEEDBACK` row 3, short quoted phrase) — a middle position between
[[hardcore-agentic-engineering]]'s engineering focus and
[[ai-pm-academy]]'s no-coding-required stance. See [[pm-vs-engineering-balance]].
