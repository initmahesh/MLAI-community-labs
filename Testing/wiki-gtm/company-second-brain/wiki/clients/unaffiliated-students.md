no company page of their own. They are captured here rather than dropped,
because one of them is a career-transition data point that matters and the other
left the most enthusiastic review in the dataset.

## Notes
"N.A." in the company field is ambiguous: it may mean unemployed, self-employed,
or simply declined to answer. It is treated here as missing data, not as a
stated status, unlike Shankar's explicit "Currently between jobs".

Both reviewers independently name the instructor's **energy** as a factor
(`MAVEN_FEEDBACK` rows 1, 7). That word appears in no competitor review in
`COMPETITOR_MAVEN_FEEDBACK`. See [[instructor-teaching-style]].
