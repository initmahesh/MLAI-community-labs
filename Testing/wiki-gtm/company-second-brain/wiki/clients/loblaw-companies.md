# Loblaw Companies Limited

## Summary
Loblaw Companies Limited is represented by Venkat krishnan Chellappa, a Senior
Product Manager who left a 5-star review. His feedback is the clearest statement
of the course's dual promise: that it equips a PM both technically and
functionally for AI work.

## Key Facts
- Student: **Venkat krishnan Chellappa**, Senior Product Manager, Loblaw
  Companies Limited (`MAVEN_FEEDBACK` row 3).
- Rating: **5 stars**, submitted 2026-07-04 (`MAVEN_FEEDBACK` row 3).
- His public review credits the build-on-your-learning teaching approach with
  aiding retention, and says the course leaves him able to discuss AI in
  real-world terms and articulate what being an AI PM requires. He states the
  course equips a student "both technically and functionally as a AI PM"
  (`MAVEN_FEEDBACK` row 3, short quoted phrase).
- From his private note (paraphrased, internal): he described the experience as
  wonderful and intense, arrived with existing PM experience, and now feels
  confident he can contribute as a PM from an AI perspective
  (`MAVEN_FEEDBACK` row 3, `private_note`).
- Canadian retail/grocery conglomerate; Senior PM seniority. The only retail
  employer in the roster.

## Notes
The phrase "both technically and functionally" is the single best articulation
in our data of the position between [[hardcore-agentic-engineering]] (technical,
light on PM) and [[ai-product-academy]] (structured, theory-heavy). It is a
customer's words, not marketing copy, and it should probably be marketing copy.
See [[pm-vs-engineering-balance]].

His before-and-after framing — experienced PM arrives, confident AI PM leaves —
mirrors [[t-mobile]]'s Sunil Gangavarapu almost exactly. Both are practising
senior PMs describing confidence transfer rather than skill acquisition. That is
the outcome our best-performing testimonials describe. See
[[career-transition-into-ai-pm]].
