# T-Mobile

## Summary
T-Mobile appears through Sunil Gangavarapu, a Senior Technical Product Manager
listed as "Ex T-Mobile". He is one of our two strongest public advocates: he
left a 5-star Maven review and independently posted a testimonial to LinkedIn
that became the second-highest-reaching post in our dataset.

## Key Facts
- Student: **Sunil Gangavarapu**, Sr Product Manager, Technical; company
  recorded as "Ex T-Mobile" (`MAVEN_FEEDBACK` row 10). He is therefore a former
  employee, not a current-client contact.
- Rating: **5 stars**, submitted 2026-06-20 (`MAVEN_FEEDBACK` row 10).
- His review values how the course connected product thinking, workflows, tools,
  evaluations, and real-world implementation — and, more significantly, gave him
  confidence that his existing product, data, API, and enterprise-systems
  background transfers into AI work (`MAVEN_FEEDBACK` row 10, paraphrased).
- **He converted review sentiment into public advocacy.** On 2026-07-04 he
  posted to LinkedIn that he had completed the Agentic AI PM Certification and
  that the program bridges the PM-to-AI gap better than other things he tried
  (`LINKEDIN_POSTS` row 2, paraphrased).
- That post drew **187 reactions and 19 comments** — second only to the founder's
  own Cohort 9 wrap-up post (`LINKEDIN_POSTS` rows 1, 2).
- It outperformed the [[ai-pm-academy]] paid-style advertisement on the same
  platform (94 reactions) by roughly 2× (`LINKEDIN_POSTS` row 3).

## Notes
Sunil is the clearest evidence in this data that our value proposition is
**confidence transfer for experienced PMs**, not entry-level upskilling — his
review and his public post make the same argument independently. This is a
different buyer from the "aspiring AI PM" audience that
[[product-manager-accelerator]] serves.

His post's reach (187 reactions) landed one day before our best YouTube
subscriber-conversion day of the window (2026-07-05, 33 subscribers on only 913
views). See [[youtube-performance]].
