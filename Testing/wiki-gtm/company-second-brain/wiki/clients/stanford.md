# Stanford

## Summary
Stanford is represented by Anja Lee, a Data PM, coach, and instructor. Hers is
the most valuable single piece of feedback in the dataset: a 5-star public
review paired with a private note containing the only substantive product
criticism we received. As a fellow instructor, her critique carries unusual
weight.

## Key Facts
- Student: **Anja Lee**, Data PM, Coach, Instructor — Stanford
  (`MAVEN_FEEDBACK` row 4).
- Rating: **5 stars**, submitted 2026-07-02 (`MAVEN_FEEDBACK` row 4).
- Her public review credits Mahesh as a seasoned practitioner in both AI

  - She wants **more PM skills content** and more time to hone them.
  - Her reasoning: not everyone is a PM, and the PM mindset matters more because

## Notes
Anja's note is the source for two of this wiki's concept pages. The lab-churn
complaint is the only operational defect recorded anywhere in the data — see
[[course-logistics-friction]]. Her request for more PM content is the direct
counterweight to our technical depth, and is the same failure mode that earned
[[hardcore-agentic-engineering]] a 3-star review from a technical PM. See
[[pm-vs-engineering-balance]].

The structural point is uncomfortable and worth stating: she gave **5 stars in
public and filed her real criticism in private**. Every public review in the
dataset is 5 stars. If public ratings are where prospects look and private notes
are where problems live, our public rating average is not a quality signal — it
is a measure of goodwill.
