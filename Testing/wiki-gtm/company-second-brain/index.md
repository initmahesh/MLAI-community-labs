## Where the data runs out

Four gaps limit almost every page above. Worth knowing before you trust a
conclusion:

1. **No pricing data** in any table, for us or any competitor. Every pricing
   statement in this wiki is inferred from language.
2. **No outcome data** — no placements, completion rates, or enrollment counts.
   Three students are mid-career-transition and none has a recorded result.
3. **No cohort column** in `MAVEN_FEEDBACK`, so reviews cannot be tied to
   [[cohort-9]] or any prior run, and no trend over time can be computed.
4. **No curriculum table.** What the course teaches is reconstructed entirely
   from what students happened to mention.
