## Before you start

- Have your **Anthropic API key** ready. Each lab asks for it once.
- Lab 1 asks you to upload two contracts (the download links are in the notebook). Labs 2 and 3 use the short AWS contract. Labs 4 and 6 ask you to upload any contract (PDF or text). Lab 5 downloads the AWS contract for you.
- Do the labs in order. Lab 2 builds on Lab 1, and Lab 3 builds on Lab 2. Lab 5 (evals and security) builds on the ideas from Labs 2 and 3. Lab 6 stands on its own, but it reuses the contract-review idea from the earlier labs.
- Run the cells from top to bottom. Don't skip ahead.
