# Claude Workshop: Build Skills That Run, Learn and Remember

Four short lessons that take you from "what is a skill?" to a skill that runs on its own every weekday, improves from your feedback, and sits next to a searchable memory of your meetings.

You build one thing across all four modules, so do them in order.

---

## What You Will Build

| Step | You get |
|---|---|
| Module 1 | The skill creator installed in Claude, and a clear idea of how a good skill is written |
| Module 2 | A working `follow-up-loop` skill that runs every weekday and emails you a summary |
| Module 3 | A feedback loop, so the skill gets better from your replies without you editing it |
| Module 4 | A second brain: a wiki built from your meeting transcripts that you and Claude can search |

---

## The Modules

### [Module 1: What Are Skills and How Do You Write a Good One?](module-1-what-are-skills/Readme.md)

What a skill is, when to use one instead of `CLAUDE.md`, the parts of a skill, and the rules for writing one Claude actually uses. You install the `allneurons-skill-creator` skill from **Customize → Add → Upload skill** and read how it is written.

### [Module 2: Get Your Follow-Up Loop Skill and Put It on a Schedule](module-2-build-and-schedule-follow-up-loop/Readme.md)

Get the `follow-up-loop` skill (upload ours, or build your own with the skill creator), test it by hand, then schedule it so it runs every weekday and emails you one summary of who to follow up with.

### [Module 3: Add a Feedback Loop So Your Skill Learns on Its Own](module-3-add-a-feedback-loop/Readme.md)

Make the skill improve over time using only your replies to its daily email. Covers the four parts of the loop (collect, review, apply, use) and a 10-minute test to prove it works.

### [Module 4: Build a Second Brain from Your Meeting Transcripts](module-4-build-a-second-brain-wiki/Readme.md)

Turn meeting transcripts from SharePoint into a linked wiki you can browse in Obsidian and that Claude can answer questions from. Covers the before and after, and how to keep the wiki fresh.

---

## Before You Start

- A Claude account where you can open **Customize** and upload skills.
- Your **email and calendar connectors** connected in Claude (Outlook or Gmail). Chat is optional. Module 2 and Module 3 need them.
- **SharePoint access** to your meeting transcripts and the free **Obsidian** app, for Module 4.

---

## How to Use These Lessons

1. Go in order. Each module builds on the one before.
2. Each lesson starts with what you will learn and ends with a quick check and key takeaways.
3. Files you need, such as `.skill` downloads and screenshots, are in each module's `assets` folder.
4. Where a lesson gives you a prompt to paste, read it once before you send it. The prompts are examples of how to brief Claude well.

Start with **[Module 1](module-1-what-are-skills/Readme.md)**.
